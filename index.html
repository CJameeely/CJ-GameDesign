DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE

COMMON MISREAD

A cat moving away does not mean it dislikes you. Staring, rushing closer, or forcing touch may be the real cause.

02

Watch appetite, activity, ears, tail, and distance. Choose food, play, space, or company for each cat.

03

PLAYABLE KNOWLEDGE
